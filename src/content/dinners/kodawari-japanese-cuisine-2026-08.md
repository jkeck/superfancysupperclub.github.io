With our size of group we were able to get a cozy privat booth. One person noted they didn't know what half the food in front of them was, and loved it anyway. The presentation was another highlight before anyone even picked up their chopsticks.

A couple of us ordered the Otsumami Box as our main, oyster included, and it delivered. One tip for next time: ask what's actually in it and get your order queued up early, since service slows down once the box is in the mix. The monkfish and bacon mochi made the rounds, and the chicken katsu came with a sauce someone called unlike anything they'd had before. The Dragon Veggie and Tempura Veggie rolls were a standout too, real effort clearly went into the vegetarian options.

Favorite dish of the night went to the chirashi don: big pieces of fish, well-seasoned rice, a little sesame, and a soy sauce lighter and more subtle than the usual pour. Close second was the katsudon, crispy grilled onions and a dashi that carried a ton of flavor.

One thing to know before you go: shoes come off once you're seated in the private room so plan accordingly!
